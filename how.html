How It Works

Four simple stages turn an unused packet or extra plant into a local connection.

1

Build your garden profile

Add garden zone, interests, pickup preferences and a short bio.

2

List what you can share

Add seeds, plants, cuttings or seedlings with clear availability.

3

Discover nearby offers

Filter by type, distance, season and exchange preference.

4

Message and complete

Agree on details, meet or arrange pickup, then record the completed swap.

03 • Exchange

Keep the conversation human
